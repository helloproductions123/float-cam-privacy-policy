# Privacy Policy for School Floating Cam

Last Updated: November 2026

The "School Floating Cam" browser extension respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it through compliance with this policy.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
The "School Floating Cam" extension requests access to your device's camera via the browser's "video" media permission. 
- This permission is used exclusively to capture a real-time visual stream and display it locally inside a native browser Document Picture-in-Picture window for your convenience.
- **No data is collected:** We do not collect, capture, store, log, or record any video, image, or audio data from your device.
- **No data transmission:** All processing happens completely client-side on your local machine. No information or media is transmitted over the internet or sent to external servers.

## 2. Third-Party Access
Because our extension processes data completely locally, no third-party services have access to your machine's hardware media streams through this software.

## 3. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be made transparently on this public page.
